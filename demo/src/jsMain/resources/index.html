Categories & priority (holiday boost)

kmatch itself never sees a category — it only scores text. Categories are your data: each menu line below is Category | Item | keywords. The left list is the library's raw text score; the right list is the same scores multiplied by your per-category boost and re-sorted. That one multiply-and-sort is the entire "priority" feature. Try it: with mocha, Caffè Mocha and Peppermint Mocha tie on text, and the Holiday boost breaks the tie.

Every score here is bit-exact with Python's RapidFuzz — verified by 3,260 golden vectors on every commit. io.github.likhithsj:kmatch
